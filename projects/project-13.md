A top-down, asymmetrical, and aggressively CO-OP game. Play as two mischievous aliens with unique abilities, the heavy-hitting JUMBO and slick SLIM, tethered together as they drag along the unconscious body of a secret agent! I was Programmer Lead on the team, most my work focused around AI with dynamic path navigation and the combat experience. Built in Unity using C# and displayed at Level Up Showcase 2026.
