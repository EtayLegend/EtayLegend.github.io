Manticeps is a platformer side scroller in which you assume the role of a parasitic virus taking control of a mantis. Fight and infect your way to spread your mutation to all life. Get stronger as you acquire more mutations on your path of assimilation and carnage. Entitled as lead VFX artist and gameplay programmer, I found myself creating and learning much about particle systems, shaders, and etc in direct application to combat gameplay specifically tied to my other work of player movement and combat.
